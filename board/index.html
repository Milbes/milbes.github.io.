Board

Meet the student leaders who helped serve the MSA community.

President

Nadine Barakat

Sister Nadine is a senior at CSUSB double majoring in Nursing and Arabic. Her favorite part of MSA is everyone coming together and growing through knowledge and shared memories. She studied abroad in South Korea and is excited for MSA this year.

Vice President

Omar Elhanafy

Brother Omar is a graduate student majoring in Social Work. He has been part of MSA for more than six years, making the organization near and dear to his heart. He is excited to serve his fellow Muslims.

Treasurer

Malik Milbes

Brother Malik is a senior studying Computer Engineering. His favorite part of MSA is finding a group of students who share his religious background, strengthening his bond with the members and, more importantly, with Allah.

Graphic Designer

Mahaa Bensufia

Sister Mahaa is a fourth-year student majoring in Graphic Design and Marketing. Her favorite part of MSA is making new friendships that bring her closer to Allah.
